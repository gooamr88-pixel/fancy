The scan result, redesigned as a place card

Five states an usher meets at the door. The direction below comes from the event's own world — engraved stationery, foil, card stock — rather than from app convention. Nothing here is in the build yet; it exists so you can reject it before it costs a release.

Content

Party, meal and note become labelled rows. Today they are one string joined by middle dots — a CSV pretending to be a layout.

Structure

Who on the left, where on the right. The usher's eye goes to the number; the guest's goes to their name. One screen, two readers.

Surface

A gradient with a light source and fine grain, not a flat fill. Flat colour is what reads as cheap on a big backlit panel.

Motion

Rule, label, name, number — a 260ms stagger. One orchestrated arrival instead of the whole screen popping at once.

Two ways out

Why red is allowed here

It is not a verdict on a person. Nobody was refused — the tablet is saying that object is not a ticket.

The single biggest content fix

Every welcome screen today ends with one string of facts glued together by middle dots. It is the detail that makes the whole screen read as unfinished.

What this changes, and what it keeps

Layout

One column becomes two. Landscape tablets have width to spare and height to spare none of — the same problem that broke the PIN pad. Identity left, destination right.

Type

The serif does names and numbers; the grotesque does labels and buttons. Today both registers sit at similar weights, which is most of why the screens read flat.

Colour

Every ground becomes a two-stop gradient with a light source and a grain overlay. Same hues, same contrast ratios — the palette is not being replaced, it is being given a surface.

Motion

A 260ms staggered entrance, and one slow foil sweep on VIP only. Everything else stays still: this is a door tool, and movement behind a queue is noise.

Unchanged

Every contrast ratio, the 64dp touch floor, the 16sp text floor, no-red-at-a-guest, and the four-state reduction. None of the safety rules move.
